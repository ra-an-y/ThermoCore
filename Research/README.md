# Purpose

Research exists to capture evidence and investigation that may support future ThermoCore Framework evolution.

It preserves the reasoning context needed to distinguish explored ideas, supported findings, research gaps, and decisions that may later inform specification work.

---

# Scope

This directory is responsible for research questions, literature investigation, evidence records, comparisons, research-gap analysis, and related exploratory artifacts.

Framework Specifications, implementation artifacts, Validation conclusions, and repository governance do not belong here.

---

# Relationship

Research may inform future work in `Documentation/`, `Framework/`, `Materials/`, and Validation-related repository areas.

Research artifacts do not become Framework requirements merely because they are recorded here. Any transition from research to authoritative specification or implementation remains represented by the applicable repository artifacts outside this directory.
